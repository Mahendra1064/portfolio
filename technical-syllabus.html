Paper I: General Technical Subject

Rajpatrankit Tritiya Shredi Civil Engineer examination, Sangh. Part II carries 50 objective marks from 50 multiple-choice questions.

Subject Distribution

  1. Structural Engineering - 5 marks
  2. Engineering Survey - 7 marks
  3. Construction Materials - 6 marks
  4. Concrete Technology - 5 marks
  5. Geotechnical Engineering - 6 marks
  6. Construction Management - 6 marks
  7. Estimating, Costing, Specification and Valuation - 5 marks
  8. Engineering Drawing - 4 marks
  9. Engineering Economics - 3 marks
  10. Professional Practices - 3 marks

Scope of Part II

The technical bank follows these ten subjects and their official subtopics. Structural Engineering covers mechanics, determinate and indeterminate structures, arches, cables and plastic analysis. Engineering Survey covers chain, compass, plane table, leveling, contouring, traversing, tacheometry, total station work, curves, areas and volumes.

Construction Materials covers stones, ceramics, bricks, lime, cement, mortar, metals, timber, asphaltic materials, paints, varnishes, polymers, soil properties and local building materials. Concrete Technology covers constituents, water-cement ratio, grades, mix design, testing, placing, curing, admixtures, high-strength and prestressed concrete.

Geotechnical Engineering covers soil formation and classification, three-phase relationships, water and index properties, rock geology, earthquakes and tunneling. Construction Management covers scheduling, contracts, procurement, cost, quality, time, safety, monitoring, evaluation, quality assurance and variations.

Estimating covers estimate types, quantity calculation, norms, rate analysis, BOQ, specifications and valuation. Engineering Drawing covers sheets, scales, site and working drawings, projections, drafting conventions, topographic, electrical, plumbing and structural drawings, freehand drawing and community buildings. Engineering Economics covers benefit-cost analysis, cost classification, sensitivity, IRR, time value, equilibrium, demand, supply, NPV and financial/economic evaluation. Professional Practices covers ethics, NEC law and regulations, professional relations, public procurement, National Building Code and building bylaws.
